How Booking Works

Booking a table at Zebra Coffee is simple and takes less than a minute. Just fill in the form below with your name, contact details, and the date and time you'd like to visit. We recommend booking at least a few hours in advance, especially on weekends, since the seating area near the window tends to fill up quickly1.

Once we receive your request, our team will confirm your reservation by phone or SMS. If you're bringing a larger group, please mention it in the special requests field so we can prepare enough seating in advance. As one visitor described it, friendly staff, a convenient location, and best of all — great coffee at a fair price —Diana Sheketasova, and we try to keep that reputation with every booking.

Reservations can be made for any day of the week, and we keep the process short on purpose — no PIN codes, no accounts, just a quick form. A booking usually includes:

  • Your preferred seating area
    • Window seats
    • Lounge area
  • Number of guests
  • Optional special requests
