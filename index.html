About Zebra Coffee

About the Coffee shop

Zebra Coffee is a cozy little coffee shop tucked away on Turkestan district, block 39, right off Dr. Park Avenue in Astana, Kazakhstan. It's the kind of place you'd easily miss if you weren't looking for it, but once you step inside, it feels like a small hidden gem in the middle of the city.

The atmosphere is warm and relaxed, with soft lighting, comfortable seating, and a calm, laid-back vibe that makes it easy to sit down with a book, chat with friends, or just take a break from a busy day. It's not overly crowded or noisy, which makes it a great spot for both quick coffee runs and longer hangouts.

Zebra Coffee offers a solid menu of classic coffee drinks — espresso, cappuccino, latte — along with a few seasonal specialties and some light snacks and desserts to go with your drink. The quality is consistently good, and the baristas clearly care about what they're making.

What I liked most about my visit was the friendly service and the overall coziness of the place. The coffee was well-made and flavorful, the staff were welcoming, and the whole experience felt genuine rather than rushed. It's a place I'd happily go back to, especially on days when I just want a quiet spot to relax with a good cup of coffee.

Coffee is what gives people vigor and energy.

— Arystan, barista Zebra Coffee, 9 september 2026
