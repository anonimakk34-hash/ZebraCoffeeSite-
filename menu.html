About Our Menu

Zebra Coffee offers a wide variety of drinks for coffee lovers and visitors looking for something refreshing. Our menu includes classic espresso-based beverages such as latte, cappuccino, americano, raf, mocaccino and flat white. Every drink is prepared by experienced baristas using carefully selected coffee beans to ensure excellent quality and flavour.

In addition to traditional coffee drinks, guests can enjoy cold beverages, frappes, lemonades and matcha tea. Many drinks can be customized with different flavours, allowing customers to choose an option that perfectly matches their preferences. Popular choices include pistachio, raspberry, mango, peach and coconut flavours.

Zebra Coffee also offers signature beverages created specifically for the cafe. Whether guests are looking for a quick morning coffee before work, a relaxing afternoon drink or something unique to try, the menu provides options for different tastes and occasions. Our goal is to serve quality drinks and create an enjoyable experience for every visitor.
