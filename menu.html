About Our Menu

At Zebra Coffee, we believe that a good coffee can make an ordinary day a little better.

Our menu combines familiar classics with creative drinks for different tastes and moods. You can choose from espresso-based favourites such as latte, cappuccino, americano, raf, mocaccino and flat white.

If you prefer something cold, we also have frappes, iced coffee, lemonades, iced tea and refreshing fruit drinks. Matcha lovers can choose between Green Matcha, Sakura Matcha and Blue Matcha.

Looking for something different? Try one of our signature drinks with unique flavour combinations created especially for Zebra Coffee.

Whether you are here for a quick morning coffee, a meeting with friends or simply a quiet break, we hope you find something you love.

Prices may change without prior notice.
